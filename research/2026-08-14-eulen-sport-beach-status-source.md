# EULEN Sport and Guardamar beach-status publication

No separate official Guardamar EULEN public endpoint was found. EULEN Sport
is the operational contractor, while the municipality-linked SafeBeach page
remains the only approved public interface for current Guardamar beach records.

Keep the SafeBeach adapter and its fail-closed freshness rules. Do not add an
EULEN provider or third-party fallback without a Guardamar-specific official
endpoint and explicit data contract.
