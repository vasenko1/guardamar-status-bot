# Ball d’Estiu 2026 source review

The recovered Ayuntamiento-branded `Ball d’Estiu` poster gives the complete
explicit 2026 schedule: June 18 and 25; July 2, 9 and 30; August 6, 13, 20
and 27; September 3, 10 and 17. Each session is 21:30–23:30 at Auditorio
Orquesta Gúmar, Parque Reina Sofía, with free admission.

The current official August poster supersedes an older archived copy that
listed 28 rather than 27 August. The runtime municipal snapshot contains the
current official dates, so no separate recurring rule is needed.
